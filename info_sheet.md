**γ-ray · X-ray · UV · visible · IR · microwave · radio**

| | γ-ray end (left) | radio end (right) |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | highest | lowest |
| Energy | highest — **strongest** | lowest — **weakest** |
| Wavelength | shortest | longest |

## Ch 8. Periodic Properties

### A. Core Concepts

<div class="nohdr" markdown="1">

| | |
|---|---|
| Penetration | An orbital reaching into the core region is less shielded → lower energy. Within a shell **s &lt; p &lt; d &lt; f** |
| Pauli exclusion | No two e⁻ carry the same four quantum numbers → 2 per orbital, opposite spins |
| Shielding | Only **core** (inner) e⁻ shield effectively; valence e⁻ shield each other poorly |

| Property | → across period | ↓ down group |
|---|---|---|
| <img src="assets/v_zeff.png" class="eqi"> | increases | ~constant |

| Group | Family | Reactivity |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | alkali metals | most reactive metals; **↑ down** the group |
| 2A | alkaline earth | reactive, less than 1A |
| 7A | halogens | reactive nonmetals; **↓ down** the group |

## Ch 9. Bonding I — Basic Concepts

### A. Lattice Energy (U)

Energy **released** when gaseous ions come together into the solid crystal.

<img src="assets/v_u.png" class="eq">

<div class="nohdr" markdown="1">

| | |
|---|---|
| ↑ ionic charge | **↑ U** — dominant, compare first |
| ↑ ionic radius | ↓ U — use only to break a tie |

</div>

- **Born–Haber cycle** — a Hess's-law loop running from the elements to the solid.

### B. Electronegativity & Bond Polarity

EN = an atom's ability to attract the electrons shared in a bond.

<img src="assets/v_en.png" class="eq">

| ΔEN | Bond type |
|---|---|
| ~0 | nonpolar covalent |
| 0.4 – 1.9 | polar covalent |
| ≥ 2.0 | ionic |

- EN increases up and to the right; F is highest. Noble gases excluded.
- Larger ΔEN → generally a **stronger** bond, and a stronger bond is a **shorter** bond.
- Bond order: triple > double > single (shorter and stronger).
